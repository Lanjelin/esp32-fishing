Two Button Fishing Pad

Flash the firmware to a LilyGO / TTGO T-Display v1.1 directly from your browser.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Firefox and Safari lack Web Serial).
  2. Plug the board in with a USB data cable.
  3. Click Install, choose the board's serial port, and wait about a minute.
  4. Pair it with your phone as an Xbox Wireless Controller.
This browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Not working? See the troubleshooting section of the README.
